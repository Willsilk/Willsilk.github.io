Case study 01 · Client

Isy's Fine Jewelry, Odessa, Texas

A family jeweller since 2014. Velocity's first paying client: social media, a new website and video, from September 2026.

The problem

About one post a week, +6 Instagram followers in 30 days, and an old WordPress site. Most of the audience was local, but nothing was built for them.

What I did

  • Daily posting, then a data-led monthly calendar
  • Reels built in code to "stay to watch" rules
  • Facebook-first pivot once the data showed where buyers were
  • New website on a custom domain, launched 27 Oct
  • YouTube Shorts channel set up and fed automatically

The insight

The owner's own story posts beat polished agency posts by 20x. So the November plan is built around capturing Isy's real stories, with a Friday "story slot", posted at 7 PM Odessa when his audience is online.
